**My Strand shelves + custom artwork — v37**

Custom icons and backgrounds for Streaming Services, Networks, Studios, Movie Collections, Kids and Family, Genres, and Moods & Vibes.

All 313 artwork folders have native TMDB or MDBList catalogs connected. Trending Movies and Trending Series are connected too. This version does not require Ultra MAX, Xperience or Trellis.

**Before importing:**
• Back up your setup and use an up-to-date Strand version.
• Add your own free MDBList API key once in Strand’s settings. Get it at https://mdblist.com/ under Preferences → API. Keep it private.
• Import ONE hero version and keep your own playback setup.

**Want different catalogs?**
You can use your personal Ultra MAX (ultramax.vip) or Xperience catalogs instead, but Strand requires your own self-hosted Trellis server for that route. Configure the provider in Trellis, connect Trellis to Strand, then use the attached AI prompt to map your exported shelves to your own collections. No shared server is provided. Keep your login details and personal links private.

Watchlist, sports and recommendations use your settings. Public catalogs may differ from the original selections. A fresh-device import has not yet been tested.
